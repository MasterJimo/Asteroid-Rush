ABOUT ASTEROID RUSH

A free arcade asteroid shooter for desktop and mobile browsers.

Asteroid Rush is a single-player survival game created by MrJim. Pilot a spaceship, dodge and shoot asteroids, clear waves and try to beat your high score. No account or download is needed.

How to play

Rotate your ship, thrust away from danger and shoot rocks. Large asteroids split into smaller ones. Each run starts with three lives; clear each wave to continue.

PC / keyboard controls

  • ← / → or A / D — rotate.
  • ↑ or W — thrust.
  • Space — shoot; hold for repeated fire.
  • Enter — start or restart.
  • P — pause or resume.
  • AUTO FIRE in Settings — optional automatic desktop firing.

Phone / tablet controls

Tap to start and use on-screen LEFT, RIGHT and BOOST. Shooting is automatic on mobile. Settings and flight data appear below the arena on small screens.

Score, waves and lives

Destroy asteroids to earn points. You begin with three lives and advance by clearing waves. The best score is saved locally in your browser; there is no online leaderboard.

Sound and settings

Select Nebula Drift or Fighting the AI, set music and effects volume separately, or mute all audio. Sound preferences are stored locally. See Data & Storage.

Availability

The game is free. Advertising and payment checkouts are not currently enabled.
