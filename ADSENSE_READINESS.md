# Asteroid Rush: AdSense readiness notes
Prepared: 20 October 2026

## Included
- Replaced the left-hand Controls section below Settings with links to About & How to Play, Privacy Policy and Data & Storage.
- Created About and Privacy pages, including real browser-storage keys and gameplay information.
- Kept current gameplay, desktop/mobile controls, audio and responsive layout.
- No ad scripts, cookie pop-up, payments or simulated consent were added.

## Publisher actions required before ads
1. Review legally required publisher identity and private contact information.
2. Verify the live hosting, About/Privacy page URLs and AdSense site eligibility.
3. Set up a Google-certified CMP where required, with working regional consent controls on desktop and mobile.
4. Disclose actual advertising partners, cookies, identifiers and data flows before ad scripts are enabled.
5. Place ads away from gameplay and touch controls; test real devices.
6. Check policies, privacy wording, navigation and accessibility. AdSense approval is not guaranteed.
